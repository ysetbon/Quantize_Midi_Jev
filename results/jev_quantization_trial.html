How each bar was tested

DataReal performanceDisklavier MIDI from the ASAP dataset (MAESTRO pianists), with annotated downbeats and the matching written score.
CodeCandidate notationsFits a tempo that follows rubato, snaps notes to grids (eighths, sixteenths, triplets, mixes) and measures timing error.
JevPick the engravingOne request per bar: up to 8 labelled options with counting ("1 e + a"), error in ms and tempo. Jev returns a choice and its confidence.
CheckCompare to the scoreEvery note's chosen position is compared to its written position. Rolled chords and grace notes count at the chord's position.
